# StyleSense.AI: User Guide

Welcome to StyleSense.AI! This guide will walk you through all the features of your new AI-powered fashion assistant.

---

## 1. Getting Started: Creating Your Account

To begin your personalized style journey, you first need an account.

1.  **Sign Up**: On the welcome screen, choose the "Sign Up" option.
2.  **Enter Details**: Provide a valid email address and a secure password (must be at least 8 characters long).
3.  **Verification**: After signing up, you will see a confirmation message. **Check your email inbox** for a verification link from us. Clicking this link is essential to activate your account.
4.  **Sign In**: Once verified, return to the app and use your new credentials to sign in.

---

## 2. The Style Quiz: Your Personalization Key

To give you the best recommendations, we need to understand your unique taste. The **Style Quiz** is the most important step!

-   **Accessing the Quiz**: Navigate to the "Style Quiz" tab.
-   **Choose Your Vibe**: Select the style that best represents you (e.g., Casual, Chic, Edgy).
-   **Select Colors**: Tap on your favorite colors from the palette.
-   **Your Profile**: Provide your gender and body type for better fit recommendations. You can also add optional measurements for a more tailored experience in future updates.
-   **Save Profile**: Once you're done, click "Save My Profile".

Your dashboard recommendations will now be tailored to your results! You can retake the quiz anytime to update your preferences.

---

## 3. Your Dashboard: The Hub of Your Style

The Dashboard is your personalized home screen.

-   **Today's Recommendations**: This is a list of outfits we think you'll love, sorted based on your quiz results and the local weather. Look for items with a "✨" tag—these are top matches for you!
-   **My Creations**: This panel shows the status of your ongoing and completed virtual try-on jobs. It's your personal AI fashion studio.
-   **Recently Tried**: A quick view of the last few items you've tried on virtually.
-   **Your Style Profile**: A summary of your quiz results.

---

## 4. Virtual Try-On Studio

See how an outfit looks on you without ever leaving your home.

1.  **Navigate**: Go to the "Virtual Try-On" tab.
2.  **Upload Your Photo**: Click the upload area to select a clear, front-facing photo of yourself. For best results, stand in a simple pose against a plain background.
3.  **Select a Garment**: Browse the collection of clothing on the right and click on an item you'd like to try. It will be highlighted with a colored ring.
4.  **Generate**: Click the "Generate Try-On" button.

Your request is sent to our AI! The process can take a minute. You can navigate away from the page and monitor its progress in the **"My Creations"** panel on your Dashboard.

---

## 5. Outfit Finder: Discover with Words

Have a specific look in mind? Describe it, and let our AI find it for you.

1.  **Navigate**: Go to the "Outfit Generator" tab.
2.  **Describe Your Outfit**: In the text box, type a description of what you're looking for. Be as descriptive as you like! Try prompts like "a professional blazer for a business meeting" or "a comfortable outfit for a weekend trip."
3.  **Find My Outfit**: Click the button to start the search.
4.  **View Results**: The AI will analyze your request and present the two best-matching items from our entire collection. You can then click "Try Me" to send them directly to the Virtual Try-On studio.

---

## 6. Style Chatbot

Have a fashion question? Ask our AI-powered StyleBot.

1.  **Navigate**: Go to the "Style Chatbot" tab.
2.  **Ask Away**: Type your question into the chat box at the bottom. For example:
    -   "What kind of shoes would go well with a denim jacket?"
    -   "Is it okay to wear bright colors to a formal event?"
    -   "What's a good outfit for the weather today?"
3.  **Get Advice**: The chatbot will provide a personalized response based on your style profile and the current context.

---

## 7. Sharing & Feedback

-   **Sharing Your Look**: Once a virtual try-on job is completed in your "My Creations" panel, a "Share" button will appear. Clicking this will open a modal where you can:
    -   Share directly to X (Twitter) or Facebook.
    -   Download the image and copy a pre-generated caption for easy sharing on Instagram.

-   **Providing Feedback**: We value your opinion! Go to the "Feedback" tab to rate your experience and leave comments. Your feedback helps us make StyleSense.AI even better.
